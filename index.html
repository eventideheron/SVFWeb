Our Farm

Rooted in the Willamette Valley

Spencer View Farm is a family-owned lavender farm nestled on the sunny side of Spencer Butte in Eugene, Oregon — where the view frames every season. We grow two exceptional varieties selected for the Pacific Northwest climate, harvested and prepared for local shops, cafés, and kitchens that value where their ingredients come from.

What We Grow

Two Varieties, One Purpose

We source our plants from Van Hevelingen Herb Nursery in Newberg, Oregon — the most diverse lavender collection in the United States.

English Lavender — Lavandula angustifolia

Pacific Blue

A rounded, spreading shrub with sweetly fragrant dark violet flower spikes. Our English variety — prized for culinary use, dried bundles, and sachets.

View at Van Hevelingen →

French Lavandin — Lavandula × intermedia

Riverina Thomas

A highly fragrant lavandin variety with robust, tall stems and exceptional essential oil yield. Our French variety — ideal for bundles, sachets, and aromatic products.
